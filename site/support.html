Help and supporting the project

Support

Two kinds of support live here: getting help when something doesn't work, and, if you want to, helping keep WrenDNS going.

Getting help

  • Email: support@wrendns.com, the one support address. A person reads every message and replies as soon as they can; there is no service level and no priority queue, and a support payment doesn't buy one (terms). Say what you run it on (a Pi, Docker, Kubernetes), the version (wrendns --version) and what you expected. A support package (wrendns support) holds what is needed with the names and addresses masked.
  • Questions and bugs: open an issue. The FAQ and the guides answer most of what people ask.
  • Moving from TelltaleDNS? The old command, paths and metric names keep working for one release. Upgrading to WrenDNS has the steps.

Stopping a monthly payment

Use the link in your Stripe receipt email, or write to support@wrendns.com and it is stopped. A monthly payment renews until you cancel it; refunds are in the terms. What is collected, and who receives it, is in the privacy policy.

Helping keep it going

WrenDNS is free and stays free. If it saves you time and you would like to help, you can chip in. It is optional, and nothing changes if you don't.
