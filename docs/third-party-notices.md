## Domain icons (the web UI)
The brand marks in `ui/src/lib/icons/brands.ts` come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) 16.38.0, which is released under the CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication. Simple Icons asks everyone who uses its marks to read its disclaimer: the marks are trademarks or logos of their owners, and are shown here only to identify the name they belong to. TelltaleDNS is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

The glyph data and the map from domains to brands are generated by `ui/scripts/brand-icons.mjs` and committed, so a build never fetches anything. To add a brand or move to another Simple Icons release, change the list or the pinned version in that script and run it (the comment at its top says how), then review the diff.
